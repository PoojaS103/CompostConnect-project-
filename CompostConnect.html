    Our Process

    From collection to community impact

    📦

    Dry Waste

    Paper, plastic & packaging

    1. 1 Collection
    2. 2 Sorting
    3. 3 Repair or repurpose usable items
    4. 4 Reuse where possible
    5. 5 Dispose of non-reusable material responsibly
    🥬

    Wet Waste

    Food scraps & organics

    1. 1 Collection
    2. 2 Separation from dry waste
    3. 3 Composting
    4. 4 Conversion into useful compost/manure
    5. 5 Use or distribution of the compost
    ♻️

    Recyclables

    Glass, metal & more

    1. 1 Collection
    2. 2 Sorting by material
    3. 3 Cleaning and preparation
    4. 4 Selling/sending materials to authorized recyclers
    5. 5 Recycling into new products
